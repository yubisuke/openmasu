# OpenMasu v0.3.0-rc.2

Configured candidate, not a published release. A tag, full platform CI at its
exact commit and verified downloadable assets are required before publication.
The current public prerelease remains `v0.3.0-rc.1`.

OpenMasu is an auditable, self-hostable mobile measurement platform for
reproducible attribution and explicit comparison. It starts as a Shadow MMP,
not a promise to replace every provider capability.

## Integrated scope since rc.1

- Selected native, platform-assigned and imported acquisition profiles, safe
  cost grain and purchase/refund/reversal calculations remain separate.
- Explicit calendar and FX definitions, same-set acquisition KPIs, mature
  retention horizons and custom outcomes preserve their saved calculation meaning.
- Guided daily setup, bounded late-input/privacy correction, receipt diagnostics
  and confirmation-bound recovery use the existing worker and dashboard.
- Saved daily analyses, previous-period views and external cohort comparison
  preserve absent, zero, undefined, incompatible and unknown values.
- Apple postback observations, registered conversion meanings and bounded
  campaign cost remain distinct from device-level attribution or actual revenue.
- Swift and Unity protected-purchase helpers retain consent and credential
  boundaries. Queued/pending is not a verified purchase or entitlement.
- Monitoring and matched backup/restore tools reuse the existing runtime.
  Frozen stable and rc.1 backups have a common forward-upgrade rehearsal.

The source and Android/iOS/Unity SDK identities are `0.3.0-rc.2`. Contract patch
definitions are through `v0.4.24`; contract wire/package identity remains `0.4.0`
and schema URNs remain `:v0.4`. This candidate adds no contract or golden change.

## Evidence and distribution

The [candidate evidence manifest](../validation/v0.3.0-rc.2-synthetic-evidence.md)
maps these paths to existing tests and records the publication requirements.
Follow [Getting started](../getting-started.md) for synthetic onboarding and
[Safe upgrade](../operations/upgrade.md) for stopped-writer updates.

The existing packager produces five Android AAR/POM pairs, a self-contained
Unity UPM archive, Swift Package source, SBOMs and checksums from one revision.
No Maven/UPM/Swift registry or binary iOS distribution is implied. Public rc.1
assets and their evidence are not relabeled as this candidate.

## Limits

All repository evidence is synthetic. Real provider permissions/connections,
devices, stores, domains, production capacity, secret custody, alert delivery
and recovery objectives remain unverified. Unity managed/Gradle and native
Simulator/emulator gates are not consumer-project export or physical-device proof.
Apple selected-schema decoding is not a verified postback binding. No
fingerprinting, iOS deferred linking or provider-side exactly-once guarantee is
introduced. This is not a production-ready or universal MMP replacement claim.
